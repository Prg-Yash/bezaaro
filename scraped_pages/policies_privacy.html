The small print

Privacy

We collect what an order needs and nothing else. We do not sell your details, and we do not send marketing you did not ask for.

What we hold

Name, delivery address, phone and email, collected to fulfil your order and to reach you about it.

Enquiry form submissions, held so we can answer you and refer back to earlier conversations.

Payment

Payment is processed on Shopify’s secure checkout. Card details never reach our servers and we never see them.

Your rights

Write to us and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. No form to fill in. An email is enough.

Questions

Anything unclear, ask before you order rather than after. info@bezaaro.com or WhatsApp.
